# Overview
I spent the summer of 2024-2025 working as a mechatronics intern at [Syos Aerospace](https://syos-aerospace.com/). My responsibilities included writing software, assembling prototypes, and assisting with test flights.

# Technical Skills

During my time I was fortunate enough to work with some incredibly talented engineers, and learn at least a smidgen of their knowledge. I learned to:

- Terminate different cable types, including ethernet, coaxial, usb, and XT60/90s.
- SMD soldering, wire-soldering, solder-soldering, an appreciation for proper fume extraction and how many times you have to burn your fingers to remember parts need time to cool down.
- How to read 'cable tables' (not sure if they have a proper name) to create wiring harnesses for avionics assemblies.
- Safety protocols for cutting and sanding carbon fiber.
- C/C++ for embedded Linux (including multi-threading).
- GPS constellation types (there's at least 6 and all use *slightly* different formats).
- How annoying leap seconds are.
- How to setup simple LAN networks.

# About Syos
Syos itself is an autonomous vehicle developer based in Tauranga, New Zealand. They develop air, land, and marine vehicles with a focus on rapid development and competitive pricing. Their vehicles are designed for several tasks including ISR (intelligence, surveillance, reconnaissance); cargo and resupply; and rebroadcast & EW (Electronic Warfare).
